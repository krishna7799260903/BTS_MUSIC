# Product Requirements Document (PRD) & Design Brief
## Project: BTS Music — BTS & ARMY Audio-Visual Experience

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Vision
**BTS Music** is a bespoke, high-fidelity audio and video streaming application crafted exclusively for the global BTS fandom (ARMY). By uniting the refined, discovery-driven user experience of platforms like Spotify with specialized K-pop community and fan-engagement mechanics (e.g., Weverse, live concert streaming, ARMY Bomb Bluetooth synchronization, and synchronized lyrics/fanchants), BTS Music delivers an intimate, culturally authentic fan sanctuary.

The product adheres to a modern, dual-mode visual language ("Borahae Purple" palette) that adapts effortlessly between immersive deep obsidian dark mode and a crisp, airy lilac-tinted light mode.

---

### 2. Objectives & Key Results (OKRs)

- **User Delight & Retention:** Provide an all-in-one ecosystem for BTS music, official solo releases, concert streams, and community curation without requiring third-party app switching.
- **Real-Time Fandom Interaction:** Drive synchronized live engagement (floating hearts, chat, and lightstick haptic/visual sync) during live concert streams and album premieres.
- **Design Excellence:** Deliver a human-made, tactile mobile UI that feels native, fluid, accessible, and compliant with modern streaming standards.

### 4. Information Architecture & Navigation

The app is built around a persistent mobile shell structure:
- **Top App Bar:** Brand Logo (`BTS Music`), Quick Theme Switcher (Dark 🌙 / Light ☀️), Global Notification Center, and User Profile.
- **Bottom Navigation (4 Core Tabs):**
  1. `Home`: Algorithmic discovery, recent listens, global chart ranking, and featured live broadcasts.
  2. `Player / Lyrics`: Full-bleed immersive audio experience with interactive lyrics and lightstick connection.
  3. `Live & Video`: Live concert broadcasts, VOD MV archive, multi-angle cams, and community chat.
  4. `ARMY Hub`: Complete discography (group + 7 solo members), milestone streaming goals, and fan-curated playlists.
- **Persistent Mini-Player Dock:** Accessible across all screens, offering rapid play/pause, track info, and scrub preview.

---

### 5. Detailed Screen Specifications & Features

#### 5.1. Home / Discovery Feed (Screen 6)
- **Personalized Greeting:** Time-aware greeting (`Good evening, ARMY 💜`) with category filter pills (All, Music, Live Concerts, Bangtan Bombs).
- **Recent Favorites Grid:** 6-tile quick-access matrix for ongoing albums (*Proof*, *Map of the Soul: 7*, *BE*, *Golden*, *Indigo*).
- **Featured Live Broadcast Card:** Hero card highlighting live stadium streams (*Yet To Come in Busan*) with live viewer counts and direct "Join Stream" CTA.
- **Curated Playlists:** Horizontal carousels for moods (*ARMY Chill Vibe*, *Hyper Workout*, *Late Night Lo-Fi*).
- **BTS Global Daily Chart:** Ranked top tracks with play counts, duration, and overflow context menus.

#### 5.2. Now Playing & Synchronized Lyrics (Screen 8)
- **Visuals:** High-res vinyl artwork with ambient purple neon glow matching the album art palette.
- **Lossless Audio Controls:** Scrub bar, waveform frequency visualizer, shuffle, loop, and tactile playback buttons.
- **Synced Lyrics & Fanchants:** Real-time synced lyric block supporting Hangul, Romanized pronunciation, and English translation with designated member markers (e.g., `JIMIN / RM`).
- **ARMY Bomb Bluetooth Integration:** Real-time lightstick sync indicator pulsing in rhythm with track BPM.

#### 5.3. Live & Video Stream (Screen 4)
- **4K HDR Video Viewport:** Ultra-low latency stream player with resolution selectors, Dolby Atmos audio toggle, and fullscreen trigger.
- **Live Fan Interaction:** Real-time synchronized chat feed with verified ARMY badges, animated floating purple hearts on tap, and viewer counters.
- **Multi-Angle Camera Switcher:** Quick-access switching between Main Stage, RM Cam, Jin Cam, SUGA Cam, j-hope Cam, Jimin Cam, V Cam, and Jung Kook Cam.
- **Live Setlist Drawer:** Tracks current song performance and previews upcoming setlist numbers.

#### 5.4. ARMY Hub & Discography (Screen 2 — Light Mode Showcase)
- **Light Theme Visuals:** Crisp, airy aesthetic utilizing lilac accents, high contrast typography, and clean surface separation.
- **Member Solo Tabs:** Filter discographies by group or individual members (RM, Jin, SUGA / Agust D, j-hope, Jimin, V, Jung Kook).
- **Visual Media Archive:** Official MVs, Dance Practices, and concert highlights with 4K UHD tags and view metrics.
- **Community Curation:** Global ARMY playlists, collaborative mixes, and community streaming targets.

### 6. Design System & Tokens Reference

- **Primary Brand Accent:** Borahae Purple (`#A855F7`), Light Violet (`#C084FC`), Deep Royal Purple (`#6D28D9`).
- **Dark Mode Backgrounds:** Surface Dim (`#15121b`), Container Low (`#1d1a23`), Obsidian (`#0f0d15`).
- **Light Mode Backgrounds:** Crisp Lilac Wash (`#FAF8FD`), Surface Container (`#F3EEF9`), Deep Plum Text (`#2D1F3F`).
- **Typography:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` — bold modern geometry with clean readability across track listings and lyrics.
- **Border Radius:** `8px` (`ROUND_EIGHT`) for structured buttons and album covers; `16px–24px` for cards and floating modal sheets.

---

### 7. Non-Functional Requirements & Technical Considerations

- **Audio/Video Latency:** Sub-second sync between audio playback and live lyrics.
- **Performance:** 60fps animations for scrubber bar, waveform visualizer, and live heart reaction bursts.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA contrast compliance across both dark and light modes, screen reader labels for music controls, and adjustable lyrics font scale.
- **Offline Mode:** Local caching for saved playlists and offline lyrics playback.

---

### 8. Release Roadmap

- **Phase 1 (MVP):** Core Audio playback, Discovery Feed, Discography directory, and Light/Dark theme switching *(Completed in Prototype)*.
- **Phase 2 (Live & Social):** Live concert streaming engine, multi-camera switching, synchronized fanchant karaoke mode.
- **Phase 3 (Hardware & Global Sync):** Web Bluetooth API integration for physical ARMY Bomb pairing, global synchronized listening parties.
