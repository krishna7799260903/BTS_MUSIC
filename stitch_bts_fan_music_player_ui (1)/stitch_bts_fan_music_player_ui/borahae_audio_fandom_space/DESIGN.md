## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-voltage, cinematic media streaming and fan-community interface engineered around the signature *Borahae* (보라해) purple heritage. It fuses Spotify-grade utilitarian audio ergonomics with an intoxicating, concert-stage atmosphere. The interface evokes intimate late-night streaming sessions, neon stadium energy, and collective devotion.

The design identity marries **Glassmorphism** with **Atmospheric Luminescence**:
- **Tactile Stage Lighting:** Deep obsidian canvases illuminated by radiant violet diffusion, soft neon aura borders, and interactive chromatic glows that respond to playback rhythm.
- **Precision Utility:** Content-first presentation prioritizing fluid navigation, thumb-zone media ergonomics, dense album scannability, and ultra-crisp typographic hierarchy.
- **Fandom Artifacts:** Premium micro-interactions, luminous pill counters, ambient light-reactive badges, and floating playback decks that feel physical yet ethereal.

## Colors

The core palette centers on an authentic electric amethyst spectrum calibrated specifically for high dynamic range OLED screens, with a complementary crisp lavender light mode.

### Dark Mode (Primary Experience)
- **Base Canvas (`#09070F`):** Deep obsidian with an imperceptible 3% violet undertone to prevent dead black flatness.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#120D1D`):** Grounded cards, sheet backings, and module groupings.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1D1530`):** Interactive pills, modal overlays, and sticky navigation headers.
- **Surface Glass (`rgba(29, 21, 48, 0.65)`): Translucent floating docks with dynamic blur.
- **Primary Violet (`#A855F7`):** Primary action buttons, active states, progress indicators, and radiant play triggers.
- **Secondary Neon Lavender (`#C084FC`):** Live badges, active song titles, track scrubbing heads, and typography accents.
- **Deep Velvet (`#6D28D9`):** Linear gradient anchors, card backdrops, and active toggle track fills.
- **Tertiary Cyan Highlight (`#38BDF8`):** Live broadcast indicators, streaming milestone tags, and high-priority fanchant cues.
- **Text Levels:** High-emphasis white (`#F8FAFC`), mid-emphasis lavender mist (`#CBD5E1`), and muted slate (`#64748B`).

### Light Mode (Crisp Daytime Alternative)
- **Base Canvas (`#FAF8FF`):** Pure crystalline lavender-tinted milk.
- **Surface Cards (`#FFFFFF`):** High-density cards with soft lilac ambient drop shadows.
- **Primary Violet (`#7C3AED`):** Deepened to preserve strict WCAG AAA contrast against light backgrounds.
- **Text Levels:** Midnight plum (`#0F0A1C`), muted violet-slate (`#475569`), and subtle line borders (`#E9D5FF`).

## Typography

The type hierarchy utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** for editorial headers and natural reading rhythm, paired with **Inter** for dense metadata, counters, track durations, and interactive controls.

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked headings (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) deliver bold, poster-like authority for album releases, discography collections, and concert countdowns.
- **Body:** Open geometric apertures provide instant legibility during fast scrolling on mobile viewports.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Inter handles tabular streaming figures, lyrics synchronization offsets, and pill badges with elevated tracking (`0.02em` to `0.06em`) in all-caps or semibold styles to maintain absolute structural clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is architected on an 8-point base rhythm, structured specifically for touch navigation and rapid thumb access.

- **Mobile Canvas:** Single-column dynamic feed with `1rem` outer margins. Content utilizes horizontal carousels with negative-margin bleed (`-1rem`) to invite fluid lateral gestures across album collections and member cards.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Compact (Mobile: < 600px):** 4-column fluid layout with dynamic bottom safe-area offsets to float the mini-player above navigation bars.
  - **Medium (Tablet: 600px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Media controls anchor to an expanded persistent side-rail or split tablet layout.
  - **Expanded (Desktop/Web: > 1024px):** 12-column layout with fixed right-hand queues, lyrics panels, and fandom interaction sidebars.
- **Rhythm Rules:** Micro-gaps between album art and track metadata use strict `space-sm` (8px). Section headline groups use `space-md` (16px) separation from carousel rails.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on luminous layering rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#09070F` base layer.
- **Level 1 (Subsurface Cards):** Background `#120D1D` with a crisp 1px border colored `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. No shadow.
- **Level 2 (Glassmorphism Tier):** Translucent backdrop `rgba(29, 21, 48, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`. Border is reinforced with a directional gradient stroke: `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.35)` at the top edge fading to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` at the bottom edge.
- **Level 3 (Floating Mini-Player & Overlays):** Elevated with dynamic violet ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(109, 40, 217, 0.45), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Aura Accents:** Key focus objects (e.g., Playing Track artwork) project an ambient blurred duplicate layer (`filter: blur(36px) opacity(0.5)`) behind the physical card to create an authentic live concert glow.

## Shapes

The system uses a balanced geometric curve language that feels soft, touch-friendly, and modern.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to track row list items, input fields, and square album thumbnails.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px):** Applied to featured album cards, artist hero containers, fandom discussion tiles, and bottom sheet dialogs.
- **Extra Large Radius (1.5rem / 24px):** Applied to the floating modal player and dynamic notifications.
- **Full Radius (9999px / Pill):** Reserved exclusively for action chips, playback control buttons, member filter pills, and live audio badges.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Play Button:** Full pill shape with a vibrant diagonal gradient (`#A855F7` to `#6D28D9`). Text/icon is pure white (`#FFFFFF`). On press: scales to `0.96` with a soft purple aura flash (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.7)`).
- **Secondary Buttons:** Glassmorphic pill backing (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) with a 1px border in `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.3)`. Text in `#F8FAFC`.
- **Tactile Scrubbers & Sliders:** 4px inactive track in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Active fill in solid `#C084FC`. Scrubber knob is a 14px circular pill in pure `#FFFFFF`, glowing with an 8px violet drop shadow on drag.

### Album & Media Cards
- **Spotify-Style Media Tile:** Aspect ratio 1:1 image rounded to `1rem` with subtle overflow shadow. Card background `#120D1D` with padding `0.75rem`. Hover or tap reveals an elevated circular purple play button in the bottom right corner with an instant spring motion.
- **Active Track Highlight:** Current playing item features a vertical 3px electric violet accent pill on the leading margin, a semi-transparent purple tinted row fill (`rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.12)`), and an animated mini audio equalizer graphic next to the duration.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Filter Badges:** Height 32px, pill-shaped. Inactive: `#120D1D` surface with muted text (`#94A3B8`). Active: solid gradient `#A855F7` to `#7C3AED` with crisp white text.
- **Member Bias Tags:** Compact 24px pills featuring subtle member-specific pastel accents nested inside dark glass containers.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Media Search Field:** Full-width rounded field (`rounded-xl`), background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`, leading violet search icon (`#A855F7`), placeholder in `#64748B`. Active focus state generates a 1px `#A855F7` outer ring and softens the backdrop to deeper obsidian.

### Fan Engagement & Community Elements
- **ARMY Bomb Sync Indicator:** Pill badge displaying live venue lightstick sync status. Inactive: charcoal ring. Connected: pulsating neon violet dot (`#C084FC`) with a radial ping animation.
- **Fanchant Sync Row:** Synchronized lyrics view where upcoming lines render at 40% opacity in lavender mist, active chant lines swell to `headline-md` in glowing electric white with dynamic purple drop shadow, and member-specific callouts appear inside neon glass badges.
